# PAAKS illustrative product imagery

Three assets were created using built-in image generation with the user-approved official logo as reference. They are **AI-created packaging mockups**, not authentic photographs of PAAKS products, staff or facilities. The original official logo remains unchanged in `public/brand/paaks-official-logo.jpeg`.

Website assets:
- `hero-concept.webp`: jar and sachet together; landscape hero.
- `sachet-concept.webp`: 500ml sachet; catalogue image.
- `dispenser-concept.webp`: 19L jar; catalogue image.

Original generated PNGs remain in the conversation's image outputs. Website versions are compressed WebP assets. To replace them with actual photos, retain the image paths or update references in `src/app/site-content.tsx`.

## Prompt briefs used

Hero: Use case product-mockup. Premium photographic-style illustrative product scene for PAAKS Purified Water. One clear blue 19-litre reusable dispenser jar with dark blue cap and one sealed 500ml water sachet beside it. White labels with the supplied official PAAKS blue/red wave and droplet logo and exact lettering. Pale ice-blue studio background, pale stone surface, soft sunlight, realistic reflections. Whole products visible with breathing room. No people, vehicles, factories, promotional headlines, certification marks, awards or fabricated claims.

Sachet: Use case product-mockup. Single sealed 500ml drinking-water sachet, upright frontal studio packaging mockup. Clear plastic edges and water inside; white label using the supplied official PAAKS logo; small 500ml text. Whole sachet visible, generous margins, square framing, pale ice-blue seamless background, grounded shadow. No jars, bottles, people, headlines or certifications.

Dispenser: Use case product-mockup. Single 19-litre reusable clear-blue dispenser jar, upright frontal studio packaging mockup, filled with water and closed with a dark blue cap. White label with supplied official PAAKS logo, small 19L text. Whole jar and cap visible, generous margins, square framing, pale ice-blue background, grounded shadow. No sachets, bottles, people, headlines, claims or certification logos.
