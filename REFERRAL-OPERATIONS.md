# PAAKS Refer & Earn — pilot operating draft

Status: first draft, not an automated rewards system. Pilot launch date and margin review are pending. The website requests registration through WhatsApp and forwards optional codes with enquiries; it does not create codes, credit balances or reserve rewards.

## Registration and attribution
Assign a unique code after approving the participant. Record referrer ID/name, contact, code, registration date and approved offer version. A newly introduced customer must supply the code before their first order is confirmed. Record attribution once per new customer, and resolve duplicate codes before accepting the order. Do not ask participants to submit someone else's contact details without permission; the new customer can contact PAAKS directly.

## Qualifying sales and margin check
Confirm the offer before the purchase. Exclude delivery charges, tax, refundable deposits, discounts and refunded amounts. Combined reward cost should not exceed 20% of contribution margin after product and variable fulfilment costs. If this gate fails, do not approve that promotional offer; establish suitable terms before ordering.

Sachets: first five bags within 30 days from first qualifying paid purchase, 2% referrer / 1% new customer. Refills: first three 19L refills within the same window, 2% / 1%. Empty bottles and events: first qualifying purchase, 2% / 1%. Cleaning: first paid completed service, 5% / 2%. Corporate: qualifying paid invoices in first 30 days, 2% / 1%, referrer credit capped at GHS 200. Bottled products remain inactive until launch and minimum-order approval. One introductory package per new customer; do not stack multiple joining offers.

## Credit ledger
Record qualifying invoice IDs, eligible net paid amount, product/service, fulfilment date, settlement date, verification date, reward rates, earned credit, approval, issue date, expiry date, redemption invoice IDs and remaining balance. Credit becomes available after fulfilment/full payment plus seven-day verification, expires 90 days after issue, and covers at most 20% of an eligible future invoice. No cash redemption, delivery payment or deposit payment. Reverse refunded rewards and notify customers of adjustments; flag previously redeemed credits for manual reconciliation.

## Pilot monitoring
Run for 90 days from the confirmed launch date. Review monthly: paying referred customers, repeat purchase rate, contribution margin after rewards, credit issued/redeemed/expired, outstanding liability, refunds and disputes. Keep registered-agent commissions separate and require an agreed written arrangement. Honour approved offers when updating future terms. Never reward Google reviews or ratings.
